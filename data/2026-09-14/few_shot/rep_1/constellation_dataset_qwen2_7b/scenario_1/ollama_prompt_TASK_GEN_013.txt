Focusing on a low priority importance task over Santa Rosa del Sur in Colombia, this is treated as a flexible schedule background survey with no rush to execute. The objective is to capture near-infrared reflection for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment to discriminate gradients. The mission aims to map the area's water bodies and soil conditions during the afternoon on the day after tomorrow, ensuring we can identify distinct water and soil characteristics for better understanding of the region. The products should be ready by then, allowing us to analyze the data at our convenience without any urgency.
