In four days, during the morning hours, we request a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission involving VNIR sensors. This will capture high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography and clear optical imagery of Prati di Tivo, Italy. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index for this area, providing valuable insights into surface reflectance and plant health. This tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.
