In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a near-infrared reflection snapshot of San Carlos de Guaroa, Colombia. This task is considered a standard operational tasking with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status. The imagery aims to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels for agricultural monitoring purposes. The deadline for delivering this data product is strictly set for the overnight period, ensuring minimal interference with local activities and optimal conditions for satellite imaging.
