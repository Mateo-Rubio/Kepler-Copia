In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a thermal mapping of the region known as Norte, located in the country of Slovenia. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, with flexible scheduling for opportunistic capture. The primary sensor used will be the TIR (Thermal Infrared) instrument, which will provide us with critical data on heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the area. This thermal mapping will serve as a valuable tool for detecting thermal anomalies and understanding heat emissions without the interference of daylight or cloud cover. As this is a background survey task, it can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if necessary.
