In response to the urgent operational execution required by our binding contract with the Malaysian government for critical payload capture, we are tasked with conducting an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology in the morning of tomorrow. This cloud-penetrating capture is essential for obtaining detailed ground geometry data, which will be used for strategic planning and infrastructure development in Bukit Mabong. The high-resolution optical snapshot of this region will provide invaluable insights into the landscape, enabling us to assess environmental conditions and support decision-making processes with accurate information.
