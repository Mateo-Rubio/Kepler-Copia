In three days, at the time of overnight, we request the capture of a low priority importance task involving a VNIR sensor over the region of Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of Foraquira. This task aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for vegetation analysis, providing insights into surface reflectance. The objective is to gather data that will help in understanding the health and distribution of vegetation in the area without altering the deadline or shifting it to another part of the day.
