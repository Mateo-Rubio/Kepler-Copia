In three days, during the afternoon, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology over Burzanella, Italia. This operational request is designated as a low priority importance task, implying that it can be accommodated within our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The goal is to perform cloud-penetrating capture for this location, which will serve as a testbed tracking task, allowing us to assess ground geometry without the constraints of weather conditions. This radar scan aims to provide critical insights into the landscape and infrastructure of Burzanella, contributing to our understanding of its unique features and potential environmental challenges.
