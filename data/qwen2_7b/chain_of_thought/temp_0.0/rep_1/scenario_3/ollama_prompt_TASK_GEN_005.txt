In three days, at the beginning of the morning, we request a low priority importance task focusing on the region of Italia, specifically the city of Porto Recanati. This mission involves conducting a routine monitoring activity using the VNIR sensor for multispectral vegetation analysis. The goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index to understand surface reflectance. This operational tasking aims to capture high-resolution optical snapshots as daylight photography, providing clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation of the area. The data products are due at the specified deadline without any alteration or extension of the time frame.
