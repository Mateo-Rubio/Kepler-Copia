This daylight photography will serve as a clear visual documentation of the city's landscape under the cover of night. As an absolute institutional priority, this "critical payload capture" involves a binding contract requirement for a mandatory directive that must be executed with urgency and without preemption. The satellite constellation is tasked with delivering thermal imagery that pierces through the darkness, capturing ground geometry and surface features that are invisible to the naked eye during the day. This active radar scan will provide us with cloud-penetrating capture capabilities, essential for understanding the city's topography in all conditions. The deadline is strict, and we must adhere to it without altering or extending the timeframe.
