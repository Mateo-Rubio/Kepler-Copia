In three days, around mid-day, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey using the VNIR sensor over Indonesia's equatorial region. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the vegetation NDVI index for an opportunistic background filler task that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The goal is to provide high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography, clear optical imagery, and true-color visual documentation of the area, focusing on the mid-day period for optimal lighting conditions.
