In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have come out, our satellite constellation will capture thermal data over Huasanó, Colombia. This mission is a low priority importance request, with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture. We aim to map heat signatures and surface temperature profiles as part of a background survey task. The deadline for delivering this data product is strictly set for an overnight period, ensuring that we capture the thermal dynamics of the region under the cover of darkness.
