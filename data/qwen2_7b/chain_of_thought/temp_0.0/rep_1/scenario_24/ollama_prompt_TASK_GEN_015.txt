In four days, at the beginning of the morning, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution active radar scan over the city of Kuala Enok, located in Indonesia. This mission is classified as a low priority importance request, which means it can be accommodated within our flexible schedule. The primary objective is to perform cloud-penetrating capture using microwave surface imaging for this area, ensuring we gather detailed information about ground geometry and any potential anomalies that might not be visible under normal daylight conditions.
