In three days, during the overnight period, our satellite constellation will capture a detailed active radar scan of Sesquilé, Colombia. This mission is classified as a low priority importance task, with flexible scheduling for opportunistic data collection. The primary objective is to perform cloud-penetrating radar imaging to gather essential surface geometry information without interference from atmospheric conditions or daylight. This operation is part of our standard baseline pipeline tracking setup, utilizing fixed parameters that represent normal operational status and will contribute to our ongoing monitoring efforts.
