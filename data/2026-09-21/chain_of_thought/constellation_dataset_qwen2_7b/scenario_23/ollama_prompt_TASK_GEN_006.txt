In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the sky is painted with hues of orange and purple, our automated satellite constellation is tasked to capture an active radar scan across the equatorial region of Indonesia. This mission, designated as a low priority importance request, involves an "evening" SAR observation designed for cloud-penetrating capture of ground geometry without the interference of daylight. The goal is to gather high-resolution microwave surface imagery that will serve as a critical tool for monitoring land use changes and infrastructure development in this region. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, this standard operational tasking aims to provide invaluable insights into the complex landscape of Indonesia during its most serene hours.
