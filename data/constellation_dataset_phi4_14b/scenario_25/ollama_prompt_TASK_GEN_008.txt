We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial mission over Prati di Tivo, Italy. The objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We need the data products delivered by the afternoon in four days at the latest, ensuring they are ingested into the pipeline before that time.
