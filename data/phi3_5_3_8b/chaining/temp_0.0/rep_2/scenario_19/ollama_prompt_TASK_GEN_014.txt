As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasking a routine monitoring operation for multispectral vegetation analysis in Norte, Italy. This standard operational tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and requires the satellite constellation to capture high-resolution optical snapshots during the afternoon within three days from now. The focus will be on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating surface reflectance indices using near-infrared reflection capabilities, essential for understanding vegetation health and dynamics in this region.
