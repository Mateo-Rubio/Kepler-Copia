In four days, at the time when the sky turns from twilight to darkness, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Sotta, France. This daylight photography will offer clear visual documentation of the city under the cover of night, providing a unique perspective on its urban landscape and surroundings. With this "non-essential background filler task", we aim to gather data that might serve as a testbed tracking task for future missions or contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We understand that this request carries a low priority importance and may be dropped or rescheduled at convenience, yet it remains a part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a comprehensive view of Earth's diverse regions.
