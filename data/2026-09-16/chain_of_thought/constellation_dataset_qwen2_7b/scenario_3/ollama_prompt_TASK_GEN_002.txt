In three days, at the dead of night when the world sleeps, we are to capture a low priority importance task - a low resolution multispectral vegetation analysis. This is a non-essential background filler task that does not require immediate attention. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels across the region in Italia, specifically focusing on the city of Ghedi. This "overnight" mission will provide us with a thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions, offering insights into the health and vitality of the vegetation without the interference of daylight's glare. The data collected will serve as a background survey to be used for opportunistic capture or as a testbed tracking task, to be dropped or rescheduled at our convenience.
