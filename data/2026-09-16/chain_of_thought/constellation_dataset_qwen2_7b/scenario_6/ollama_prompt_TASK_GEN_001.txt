In three days, around mid-day, our satellite constellation is tasked with capturing a high-resolution active radar scan over San Vicente del Caguán, Colombia. This critical payload capture involves cloud-penetrating microwave surface imaging to provide detailed ground geometry data, essential for operational planning and environmental monitoring in this region. As an absolute institutional priority, this mission is executed with the utmost urgency and importance, ensuring that the data products are delivered on time without any possibility of being preempted or delayed. The data collected will serve as a binding contract requirement, providing invaluable insights into the area's topography and surface conditions.
