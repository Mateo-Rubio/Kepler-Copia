In four days, at the beginning of the morning, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule "opportunistic survey" or "testbed tracking task". This involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels through near-infrared reflection to map water body boundaries and evaluate soil moisture conditions. The mission will be conducted in Moncalieri, Italia, using a routine monitoring approach for baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data collection is aimed at providing insights into the ecosystem health without an urgent operational execution or contractual requirement.
