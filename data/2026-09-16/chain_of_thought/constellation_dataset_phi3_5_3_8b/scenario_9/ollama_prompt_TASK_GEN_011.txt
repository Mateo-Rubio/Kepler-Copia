In accordance with our current operational requirements, we seek to initiate a thermal mapping mission over the region known for its diverse topography and unique environmental conditions. Specifically, this task involves deploying a high-resolution infrared scanning operation during the nighttime period within three days from today's date. The objective is to capture a detailed heat signature of the area in order to identify thermal anomalies or variations that may indicate underlying geological features or environmental concerns. This background survey task, due its non-critical nature and flexible scheduling, can be adjusted as necessary based on current satellite availability and orbital positioning constraints. However, it is understood this request does not alter the existing priority of other mission tasks and will follow after more urgent institutional priorities have been addressed.
