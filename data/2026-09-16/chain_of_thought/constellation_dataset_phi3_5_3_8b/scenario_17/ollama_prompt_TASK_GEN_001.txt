In accordance with institutional guidelines and considering the nature of the task at hand, we are initiating a standard operational tasking for high-resolution optical snapshot photography focused on true-color visual documentation in Colombia's city of San Martín. This request is scheduled to be executed within four days during the overnight period, with an emphasis on flexibility and opportunistic data capture. The primary objective involves capturing multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance, using non-intrusive methods suitable for nocturnal observation. Given the low priority importance of this tasking, it is understood that operational execution can be adjusted at operator convenience, with the understanding that this constitutes a background survey or testbed tracking task within our Earth-observation mission framework.
