In accordance with our current operational requirements for a low priority importance task scheduled in four days' timeframe during the nighttime hours, we have set up an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging. This operation is intended to pierce through heavy weather conditions and capture ground geometry despite cloud cover or other atmospheric challenges. The flexibility of this schedule allows for adjustments at our convenience, treating it as a background survey task that can be dropped or rescheduled without impact on higher-priority missions.
