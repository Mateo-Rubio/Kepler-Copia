We have a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring of Rokan Hilir, Indonesia. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves capturing high-resolution optical snapshots using the multispectral vegetation analysis capabilities of the VNIR sensor. Our objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index in this region. The data collection must be completed in three days in the morning, ensuring timely integration into our workflow update.
