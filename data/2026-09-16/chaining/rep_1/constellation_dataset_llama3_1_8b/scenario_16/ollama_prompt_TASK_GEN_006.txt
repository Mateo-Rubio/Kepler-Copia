We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over the region of Mulya Bakti in Indonesia. This is a standard commercial tasking with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, and we can drop or reschedule at convenience if needed. The data must be delivered within three days overnight.
