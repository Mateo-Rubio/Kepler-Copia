The Canelo area in Colombia requires a standard operational tasking for multispectral vegetation analysis. We are looking to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) along with surface reflectance measurements. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, featuring fixed parameters indicative of normal operational status. The data collection must be executed by the day after tomorrow in the morning to ensure timely delivery and integration into our ongoing environmental assessment efforts.
