We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial satellite mission over Bangka Selatan, Indonesia. This routine monitoring forms part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The primary objective is to conduct near-infrared reflection imaging aimed at water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. It's essential to capture the subtle gradients in this region, which will provide valuable insights into the local environmental conditions. The task must be completed in three days in the morning to ensure data availability aligns with our workflow update schedule.
