In the captivating landscape of Northern Italy, our mission's priority level is set to a low importance, allowing for a flexible schedule that accommodates opportunistic capture. As we embark on this task, our primary sensor, the VNIR (visual near-infrared), will be utilized for a detailed assessment of the region's vegetation health. This involves a meticulous analysis of chlorophyll absorption levels and the calculation of the NDVI index, providing us with invaluable insights into surface reflectance patterns.

With the target day set for "the day after tomorrow", during the afternoon, our focus remains on capturing essential data without the constraints of a strict deadline. This background survey will serve as a testbed tracking task, offering a glimpse into the region's ecological dynamics without the urgency typically associated with higher priority missions. We look forward to delivering this information in time for informed decision-making, ensuring that Northern Italy's environmental health is preserved and monitored effectively.
