To ensure comprehensive coverage of environmental changes in Arnoldstein, Austria, our Earth-observation mission operator has requested a low-priority task focused on capturing near-infrared (NIR) reflection patterns overnight in three days. This mission aims to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels opportunistically, contributing to background survey efforts without compromising critical or baseline operational tasks. The high-resolution optical snapshot of Arnoldstein will serve as a crucial reference for understanding local environmental dynamics, complementing existing datasets with unique insights into night-time conditions that are essential for comprehensive monitoring and analysis.
