In anticipation of capturing a comprehensive view of the region's environmental health, we're scheduling an observational mission for the day after tomorrow in the morning. This "background survey" aims to contribute to our ongoing efforts in understanding the ecological dynamics of this area without imposing any constraints on operational execution or asset allocation.
